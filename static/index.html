Method & sources · how the estimate works
Reviewed kWh × kg gas per kWh = estimated kg of emissions. MWh is converted to kWh. We block kVAh and unspecified units. The default factor is configured by your platform administrator. Verify its source, gas basis, region and applicable year before use. A CO₂ factor is not a full CO₂e inventory. The calculation excludes upstream and transmission/distribution losses.
