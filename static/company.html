How totals are built

  1. Attribute records to a company, facility and reporting period.
  2. Keep source quantities and the exact factor used.
  3. For electricity: normalize MWh to kWh, then multiply by kg gas/kWh.
  4. For custom factors: multiply quantity by kg gas per declared input unit.
  5. Include only reviewed activities in dashboard totals; keep CO₂ and CO₂e separate.
  6. Editing a reviewed activity returns it to draft and records the prior version.
